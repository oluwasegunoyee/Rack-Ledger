08 — Frequently asked

Good questions
deserve clear answers.

Everything starts with fit. If your question isn't here, include it in your project enquiry.

What does a Shopify project start at?

Our enquiry form starts at $500 so we can understand smaller and larger opportunities. The final scope is priced around the work required, not an arbitrary package.

Can you redesign an existing Shopify store?

Yes. We can work from an existing theme, redesign the experience, rebuild sections or develop a more substantial custom storefront.

Do you work with brands outside Nigeria?

Yes. Rack & Ledger is positioned for remote work with ambitious ecommerce brands worldwide.

Can you work with an existing brand identity?

Absolutely. We can preserve what is working and translate the identity into a more cohesive ecommerce system.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the brief, assess fit and reply with the clearest next step. A project call can follow when useful.

Do you offer ongoing Shopify support?

Yes. Support can cover refinements, new sections, landing pages, merchandising improvements and ongoing storefront development.
