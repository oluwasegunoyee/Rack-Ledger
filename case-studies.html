These portfolio concepts demonstrate how Rack & Ledger approaches different commerce categories. They are intentionally presented as concept work rather than fabricated client results.

01 / Northline Objects

Editorial structure for a product-led brand.

The design uses large type, restrained navigation and a clear product rhythm to make a lifestyle catalogue feel more like a magazine without losing its shopping utility.

Design principle

Desire first. Friction last.

Strong art direction establishes emotional context before the customer reaches the commercial decision.

02 / Form House

Quiet luxury without hiding the product.

A calmer visual language gives premium home products space to breathe while keeping price, product information and purchase actions discoverable.

UX principle

Reduce the cognitive load.

Every section should answer a question or move the shopper closer to confidence.

03 / Atelier Supply

Utility, hierarchy and fast discovery.

For product-rich catalogues, the interface needs strong merchandising logic. Category cues, price visibility and collection structure do the heavy lifting.

Commerce principle

Make comparison effortless.

Customers shouldn't have to fight the interface to understand the difference between products.

04 / Morrow Studio

Story-led commerce for considered objects.

The storefront connects brand narrative and product discovery so the story strengthens the commercial proposition rather than competing with it.

Brand principle

Story should sell.

The strongest editorial moments reinforce product value, differentiation and trust.
